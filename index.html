    ✦ About the project

    Technology with a purpose.

    CivicShield is an experimental civic-tech concept designed around a simple principle: helping people turn concerns into practical next steps.

    ◈

    The problem

    People may struggle to identify the right organization, understand reporting procedures, or structure a complaint about a community issue.

    ✦

    Our solution

    An accessible interface that analyzes a problem using local demo logic and turns it into a practical action plan and editable report.

    ⌘

    Technology

    HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with a local rule-based classification engine. No backend or external API is required for this demonstration.

    ↗

    Future vision

    Verified service directories, multilingual AI assistance, accessibility testing, and integrations with legitimate civic reporting channels.
